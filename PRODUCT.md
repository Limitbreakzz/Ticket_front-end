# Product

## Register

product

## Users
- **User (Employee)**: Internal organizational employees who need to request IT support or report hardware/software issues. They need to create tickets, track progress, add comments/attachments, and view their history quickly without distraction.
- **Admin (IT Staff)**: Support agents and system administrators who manage, assign, and resolve tickets, manage users and configurations, and view operational dashboards.
- **Manager**: Department or team leaders who monitor ticket activity and team performance, access management-level reporting, and approve specific workflows (like access/hardware requests) when required.

## Product Purpose
Ticket Hub is a clean, reliable, and highly efficient IT Helpdesk Ticket System. It provides internal teams with a structured pathway to resolve operational IT issues quickly and transparently, ensuring minimal downtime and clear communication.

## Brand Personality
- **3-Word Personality**: Professional, Efficient, Reliable
- **Voice & Tone**: Confident, helpful, clear, and direct. The interface should focus on clarity and action, avoiding decorative elements or playful animations.
- **References**: Jira Service Management, Linear, Notion, GitLab, Atlassian Design System, shadcn/ui.

## Anti-references
- **Generic Admin Templates**: Overloaded with colored graphs, widgets, and unnecessary decorative metrics.
- **Table-Heavy layouts**: Large unformatted spreadsheets that increase cognitive load.
- **Overly bright or saturated color schemes**: High-contrast, flashing, or neon elements that induce fatigue.
- **Excessive visual effects**: Blurs, glassmorphism, or gratuitous slide-in animations.
- **Outdated enterprise dashboards**: Crowded, blocky, and multi-colored panels.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity Over Complexity**: Maximize readability of ticket status, details, and conversation threads. Simplify data tables and forms.
- **Focused Execution**: The interface should support fast operations (assigning, updating status, commenting) with clear focus indicators and intuitive controls.
- **Thai-First Localization**: Seamless typography alignment and structure optimized for Thai script rendering, with structured hooks for English translation in future phases.
- **Workflow Continuity**: Minimize screen jumps and modal stacking. Maintain visible and consistent context when reviewing and actioning tickets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Keyboard Friendly**: Robust focus ring styling and sequential tab navigation.
- **Enhanced Contrast**: Text-to-background contrast meeting WCAG AA requirements, specifically avoiding light gray text on soft slate.
- **Responsive Layout**: Designed to adapt seamlessly across dynamic desktop viewports and mobile screens.
- **Reduced Motion**: Respects prefers-reduced-motion CSS queries to prevent animation-related cognitive strain.
